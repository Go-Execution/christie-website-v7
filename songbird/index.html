The world of Songbird

To lose your voice.
And find it again.

Songbird is a heartfelt, imaginative project that brings together original music, storytelling, a musical, and a book so children can explore their emotions, discover their inner strength, and reconnect with the beauty of life.

At its center is Songbird, a character shaped by Christie’s deeply personal journey of losing one’s voice and finding it again. Her experiences invite children to see that their feelings deserve to be heard and their unique gifts can become a source of strength, connection, and love.

Through music, imagination, mindfulness, and a relationship with the natural world, Songbird asks children to listen to themselves, to one another, and to nature. Kindness, compassion, emotional intelligence, and presence guide the journey.

From the world of Songbird

Every song opens
a new door.

In Fairy Mindful Butterfly, children slow down and discover the beauty of the present moment. I Love the Dogs explores empathy, gentleness, and the unconditional love we share with animals. Sleep Baby Tight celebrates a mother’s tenderness and the comfort of a peaceful lullaby.

These stories invite children to experience music emotionally and creatively, whether they find a moment of calm, a feeling of belonging, or the courage to express themselves.
