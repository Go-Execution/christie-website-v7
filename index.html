Welcome to the world of Songbird

Where listening becomes
a kind of magic.

Songbird is an original creative world by singer-songwriter, pianist, visual artist, and music educator Christie Kanska.

Through music, storytelling, a children’s book, and a musical, Songbird invites children and families to explore their emotions, discover their inner strength, and reconnect with the beauty of life.

At its heart is a deeply personal journey: losing one’s voice and finding it again. It is a celebration of kindness, mindful listening, imagination, and the truth that every one of us has something valuable to share.

The artist behind Songbird

Created from a life
lived through music.

“I want to create music that is genuinely human, emotionally honest, and lasting—music that children can enjoy today and carry with them as they grow.”

Born in Yerevan, raised in Thessaloniki, and now at home in New York, Christie Kanska has devoted more than 35 years to helping students develop their voices, musical abilities, and confidence.
