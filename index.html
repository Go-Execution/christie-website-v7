Meet Christie Kanska

A life composed
across cultures.

Vocalist, pianist, composer, choral director, singer-songwriter, and music educator.

Born in Yerevan, Armenia, Christie began playing piano at seven. After classical piano, jazz and contemporary music studies, and a Fine Arts degree, she moved to Greece in 1995 and joined the vocal faculty of the New Odeon of Thessaloniki and Filippos Nakas Odeion Conservatory.

A Berklee College of Music scholarship brought her to Boston, where she completed a Bachelor of Music in Professional Music in 2004. Her journey has since taken her from concert halls and jazz clubs to classrooms and recording studios across Europe, India, the Caribbean, and the United States.

Featured recording

A dream shaped in Latin jazz.

Le Rêve — The Dream blends Latin Jazz, Bossa Nova, Fusion, and Christie’s own musical language. Its eleven tracks include her original instrumental “Le Rêve,” Zdenek Kansky’s “For Christie,” and new arrangements of beloved Brazilian standards.

The album features Christie on vocals and keys, Zdenek Kansky on electric and acoustic bass, and an international ensemble of jazz musicians.

Founded by Christie Kanska and Zdenek Kansky, ZCK Music Production works with songwriters who write lyrics only as well as artists bringing complete songs. Its professional team develops music, arrangements, recordings, and release-ready creative materials.
