The artist

Music without
borders.

Music carries memory, imagination, and human connection.

Born in Yerevan and shaped by musical lives in Greece, Boston, India, Europe, and New York, Christie brings classical training, jazz freedom, and a teacher’s instinct for connection to everything she creates.

Her work moves naturally between the concert stage, recording studio, classroom, and children’s choir—always centered on honest expression and the belief that music helps people find their own voice.
