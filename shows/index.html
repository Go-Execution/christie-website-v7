Earlier years

From intimate clubs to international stages

2020

New York and Boston appearances

Performances and musical work continued as Christie settled in New York, while her original song “Stay Home” emerged during the global lockdown.

2019

Le Rêve tour

Concerts supporting Le Rêve — The Dream brought Christie and her collaborators to leading jazz venues in India, with radio play reaching audiences internationally.

2018

Chennai and the British Consulate

Christie served on the voice faculty at Swarnabhoomi Academy of Music and performed at a concert organized by the British Consulate in Chennai.

2006

World Jazz Group

A European concert run through the Czech Republic, Slovakia, and Austria included an appearance at Prague’s renowned Reduta Jazz Club.

1997

Thessaloniki festivals

Christie appeared on ET3 television and at the Odeion of the Ancient Market, presenting arrangements of Armenian and Greek folk music.
