A Lifetime of Love, Compassion, and Rescue

Christie’s original song grows from childhood memories and a lifelong commitment to caring for dogs.

01 / The beginning✦

From Armenia to Greece

A love that began early.

My story began in Armenia when I was just 10 years old. I would find stray dogs, wash the dirt from their coats, feed them, play with them, and eventually try to find loving homes for them. Even as a little girl, I felt a deep responsibility to care for these beautiful creatures.

As I grew older, my love for dogs continued, and I became involved in caring for stray dogs and supporting dog shelters in Greece.

A story close to my heart

The dog who found her strength.

One particular story will always remain in my heart.

One day, my aunt, the poet Sofia, brought me a beautiful dog whose leg was severely injured and bleeding. She was still alive, but she was suffering terribly. I immediately felt a deep connection with her and did everything I could to help.

Sadly, the injury was so severe that we eventually had to make the difficult decision to amputate her leg. I stayed by her side, helping her through the painful recovery and making sure she received the care and pain medication she needed.

Slowly, she recovered, found her strength again, and blossomed into a magnificent dog, with the beautiful appearance of a Husky and German Shepherd mix.

Before I left for America to continue my studies, I arranged for her to live in a loving foster home in Athens, where two wonderful ladies welcomed her and continued caring for her.

02 / The song♪

Compassion in every note

Why I wrote this song.

Even after experiencing hearing loss and undergoing two surgeries on my left ear, my love and compassion for dogs never changed. Throughout my life, I continued to help, support, and care for dogs, including those belonging to my students.

I have collected many photographs and stories from these experiences, and I would love to share them here. Each dog has a story, and each one has left a special place in my heart.

My lifelong connection with dogs is one of the reasons I wrote my original song, I Love the Dogs. The song comes from a genuine place of love, childhood memories, compassion, and personal experiences.

Through this page, I hope to share not only my music but also the stories behind it. I believe that kindness toward animals teaches us something beautiful about humanity.

Every dog deserves love, dignity, kindness, and a safe place to call home.

Christie Kanska
