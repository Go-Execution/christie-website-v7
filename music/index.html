Studio album · 2019

Le Rêve
The Dream

An eleven-track journey through Latin Jazz, Bossa Nova, Fusion, and original music.

Arranged by Christie Kanska and Zdenek Kansky, the album pairs Christie’s expressive voice with vibrant arrangements and international musical influences. It includes music associated with João Donato and Antônio Carlos Jobim alongside original compositions.

About the dream

“Music has the power to make our dreams come true.”

The album’s title reflects a lifelong dream realized through years of performance and creative partnership. Its sound carries pieces of Armenia, Greece, Boston, Prague, India, and New York—places where Christie’s musical language continued to grow.

Following its release, Le Rêve was heard on radio in London, Massachusetts, Greece, and the Czech Republic, and Christie brought the music to jazz venues across India.
