News from a life in music

2019 · Album release

Le Rêve — a dream realized

Christie’s studio album brings together Latin Jazz, Bossa Nova, Fusion, and original music. Created with Zdenek Kansky and a circle of gifted musicians, it is both a musical journey and an expression of gratitude.

Read the Jazziz feature ↗
2020 · Original song

Stay Home

Written during lockdown, “Stay Home” reflects on the beauty of nature, closeness of family, gratitude for home, and our responsibility to protect the planet.

Watch the video ↗
2021–2023 · Recognition

Education and mentorship

After receiving a Gold Medal in Voice and Royal Conservatory recognition, Christie continued as a Steinway educator and joined the 2023 Grammy U Mentor program.

2022 · Carnegie Hall

Carmina Burana

Christie performed Carl Orff’s Carmina Burana with the Cecilia Chorus of New York at Stern Auditorium / Perelman Stage.

Archive · 2020

Tchaikovsky Piano Competition

Christie’s original news archive also highlighted the International Tchaikovsky Piano Competition—one of classical music’s best-known competitions.
