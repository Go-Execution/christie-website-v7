Christie’s approach

Technique gives
imagination wings.

Every student is different. Lessons are shaped around individual goals, experience, learning style, and musical curiosity.

Christie teaches the technical foundations that allow expression to grow freely. Students develop control and confidence while learning to make thoughtful musical choices of their own.

Her instruction welcomes styles including Pop, R&B, Jazz, Musical Theatre, Folk, and Classical music.

Areas of study

A complete musical foundation

01

Voice

Diaphragmatic breathing, vocal exercises, healthy execution, tone, range, interpretation, and the technique needed for confident singing.

02

Piano

Individually structured lessons for beginners through advanced students, combining reading, technique, repertoire, and musicianship.

03

Performance

Stage presence, song interpretation, theatre preparation, audition skills, and practical tools for communicating with an audience.

04

Creativity

Students are encouraged to compose, improvise, and develop their own musical ideas alongside solid technical skills.

05

Royal Conservatory

Curriculum-based preparation through the Royal Conservatory Music Development Program, tailored to each student’s path.

06

Scholarships

Partial scholarships may be available for motivated and gifted students who show commitment to their musical development.

The goal

More than lessons—musical independence.

Christie’s aim is to help students understand how music works, how their instrument responds, and how to practice with purpose. Technique and imagination develop together so that students can become expressive, curious, self-directed musicians.
