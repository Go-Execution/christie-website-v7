About Christie

A life in music.

Vocalist, pianist, composer, choral director, singer-songwriter, music educator, and producer—an artistic journey spanning cultures and continents.

From Yerevan to New York

Music has always been
the connecting thread.

Born in Yerevan, Armenia, Christie began piano at seven and built a career shaped by classical discipline, jazz freedom, and a deep love of teaching.

After studying classical piano, Jazz and Contemporary Music, and earning a Fine Arts degree, she moved to Greece in 1995. There she joined the vocal faculties of the New Odeon of Thessaloniki and the Filippos Nakas Odeion Conservatory.

A Berklee College of Music scholarship brought Christie to Boston, where she completed a Bachelor of Music in Professional Music in 2004.

A journey across cultures

Selected chapters

Early years

Armenia and Greece

Christie sang as a soloist with classical, modern, and jazz ensembles, performed with the National Chorus of Yerevan, and conducted the Children’s Chorus of the Greek Embassy in Armenia. In Thessaloniki she appeared at festivals, on ET3 television, and at the Odeion of the Ancient Market.

2005–2006

Recording and touring

Her Boston album Who Needs Love featured Jamey Haddad, Leo Blanco, Spiros Exaras, Rick DiMuzio, and Zdenek Kansky. Tours with the World Jazz Group followed across the Czech Republic, Slovakia, and Austria, including Prague’s celebrated Reduta Jazz Club.

2014–2019

ZCK Music Production and Le Rêve

Christie and Zdenek founded ZCK Music Production. After serving on the voice faculty at Swarnabhoomi Academy of Music in India, she released Le Rêve — The Dream, an eleven-track blend of Latin Jazz, Bossa Nova, Fusion, and original music.

2020–today

New York, recognition, and the next song

Now based in New York, Christie is a Grammy Recording Academy member, Steinway educator, 2023 Grammy U Mentor, and Carnegie Hall educator. Her work has included Harmony Program children’s choirs, the Cecilia Chorus of New York, and continued development of children’s music and her original song “I Am in Control.”

Artist and educator

The stage and classroom
belong together.

Christie’s work moves naturally between performance, composition, choral direction, and education. She has taught through the Royal Conservatory curriculum, led youth ensembles, and performed in concert halls, jazz clubs, consulates, and festivals.

In 2021 she received a Gold Medal in Voice and recognition from the Royal Conservatory. In 2022 she performed Carl Orff’s Carmina Burana at Carnegie Hall with the Cecilia Chorus of New York.
