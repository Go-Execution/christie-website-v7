ZCK Music Production

From idea
to finished song.

Creative and practical support for songwriters and artists—from the first lyric or rough recording to a polished musical release.

Make the music real

Your words already have
a song inside them.

ZCK Music Production helps lyric writers hear their words become complete songs and helps musicians turn rough recordings into professional demos.

Christie Kanska and Zdenek Kansky founded ZCK Music Production in 2014. Their approach combines musical experience, thoughtful artist development, and a trusted network of professional musicians chosen to complement each project.

The studio also supports the path beyond recording, including advance releases to media, album artwork, and distribution guidance.

How ZCK can help

Support for every stage of the creative process

01

Lyrics to music

Melody, harmony, arrangement, and musical direction for writers who have words but need a complete song.

02

Demo production

Turn a rough voice memo or early recording into a clear, professional demo ready to share.

03

Session musicians

Bring in experienced professional players whose sound and sensibility fit the needs of your song.

04

Artist development

Creative guidance designed to strengthen your material, identity, presentation, and next steps.

05

Release support

Advance release planning, media outreach, album presentation, artwork, and distribution guidance.

06

Music education

ZCK is affiliated with the Royal Conservatory Music Development Program and shares a deep commitment to musical growth.
